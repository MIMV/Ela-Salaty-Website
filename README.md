## What is Ela-Salaty?

Ela-Salaty (إلى صلاتي — *"towards my prayer"*) is a prayer companion for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, and Vision Pro. It does five things — and obsesses over every one of them:

- **Prayer times** — accurate to the minute, anywhere on earth
- **Qibla** — a compass that always points home to the Kaaba
- **Athkar** — morning, evening, after-prayer, and wudu, beautifully laid out
- **The cinematic Athan** — a full Athan in hand-tuned themed scenes, for when you can't make it to the mosque
- **Living themes** — backgrounds that wake with the day: Fajr light, Maghrib clouds, Ramadan lanterns, the Hassan II minaret at Isha

Built for one job. Nothing else.

## What makes it different

### A sanctuary, not a feed
No accounts. No ads. No analytics. No third-party trackers. Nothing leaves your phone — because nothing has to.

> Verified by Apple App Privacy: **Data Not Collected.**

### Built into the iPhone, not just on it
- **Cinematic full Athan** — watch a full Athan in beautiful, themed scenes
- **Living themes** — backgrounds that shift with day and night
- **Widgets, everywhere** — Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, Control Center, Dynamic Island, Live Activities
- **Apple Watch** — next prayer on your wrist as a complication; Qibla on glances
- **Hey Siri** — "Hey Siri, Ela-Salaty." She knows what to say, and asks if you prayed
- **Prayer times worldwide** — search any city, save it, see local + your time side-by-side, make a calendar appointment in one tap
- **Full Athkar** — morning, evening, after-prayer, wudu — the complete collection
